Frequently Asked Questions

What is Coast FIRE?

Coast FIRE is a financial independence strategy where you save aggressively now until your investments have grown enough that compound interest alone will carry you to retirement. Once you reach your "Coast FIRE number," you can stop saving entirely and let the market do the rest.

How accurate is this calculator?

This calculator uses standard financial assumptions: 7% average annual return (based on historical US stock market performance) and a 4% safe withdrawal rate (based on the Trinity Study). Your actual results will vary based on market timing, investment choices, inflation, and other factors. This is for educational purposes only, not personalized financial advice.

Does this include Social Security?

No, this calculator assumes zero Social Security income. If you expect Social Security benefits in retirement, your Coast FIRE number will be lower since those benefits can cover 30-50% of typical expenses. Check your estimate at ssa.gov/my-account.

What if I want to retire before age 65?

The Coast FIRE strategy is specifically designed for people planning to work until traditional retirement age (65+). If you want to retire earlier, you'll need a higher savings rate or different strategy like Lean FIRE or Barista FIRE.

Why stop at Coast FIRE?

Coast FIRE gives you optionality. You're not forced to work solely for money anymore. You can choose lower-paying jobs that align with your passions, take career breaks, or work part-time—all without jeopardizing your retirement security.

How does inflation affect this?

The calculator assumes real (inflation-adjusted) returns of 7%. This is the historical average return of US stocks after inflation. If you invest internationally or in bonds, your actual returns may differ. Consider this when planning.

What's the difference between Coast FIRE and regular FIRE?

Traditional FIRE requires you to save enough to never work again. Coast FIRE is a milestone: once you reach it, you can ease off savings entirely. Regular FIRE = complete financial freedom. Coast FIRE = "I've got this covered, now I can choose whether to work."

Is 7% too optimistic for returns?

The 7% figure is based on the S&P 500's historical performance from 1926-2024. However, past performance doesn't guarantee future results. You could experience poor returns when you need them most (sequence of returns risk). Consider using a conservative number like 5-6% if you're risk-averse.

Can I use this with crypto or alternative investments?

The calculator assumes traditional stock market returns. Crypto, real estate, and other alternatives have higher volatility and may not provide the same reliable long-term growth. If using non-traditional assets, adjust your expected return rate accordingly.
