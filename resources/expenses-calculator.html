About These Numbers:
The Full FIRE Target is what you need in your portfolio to withdraw 4% annually. The Coast FIRE Number is how much you need TODAY (at current age) so that compound growth alone will reach the Full FIRE Target by your target retirement age. Once you have the Coast FIRE number invested, you can stop saving aggressively and let compounding work for you until traditional retirement age or whenever you choose to retire.
