About This Checklist

This checklist guides you through the journey to Coast FIRE. Each phase builds on the previous one, maximizing your compound growth potential while maintaining flexibility.

Remember:
• Track progress annually
• Don't panic-check portfolio value monthly
• Every year you invest early is worth multiple years of delayed investing
• Lifestyle choices matter—resist creeping up your spending
• Use the calculators at coastfirewhen.com for precise numbers
